  the chain operates on.

  paths; nothing from S0 constrains their shape beyond hash/height
  fields conventional for a header store.

  ready to consume.

  `block:tip` is wired in `WalletHub.SubscribeToBlockTip`.

  no emitter in W1 (subscription group `block:reorg` registered
  but quiet).

  `PeerTelemetry` snapshot ready; W6 swaps the construction site
  inside `PeerManager` to ship the production sink.
- W5: `BroadcastReceiptDto` shape frozen; server-method signatures
  (`IWalletServer.Broadcast`, `BroadcastTracked`) intentionally
  not frozen — W5 may collapse them.
